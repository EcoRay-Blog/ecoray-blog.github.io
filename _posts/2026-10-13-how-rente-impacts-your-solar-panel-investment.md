### Why Rente Matters When You Buy Solceller

Choosing to køb solceller is a big step towards energy independence and a greener future. But for most private home owners and businesses in Denmark, Sweden, Norway, and the USA, financing is a key part of the process. Here’s how rente comes into play:

- **Monthly payments:** The rente on your loan or financing package affects your monthly costs, which impacts your overall return on investment.
- **Total cost:** Even with solceller og tilskud or government incentives, higher rente means you might pay more over the lifetime of your solcelleanlæg.
- **Payback period:** A lower rente shortens the time it takes for your solceller to pay for themselves through energy savings.
