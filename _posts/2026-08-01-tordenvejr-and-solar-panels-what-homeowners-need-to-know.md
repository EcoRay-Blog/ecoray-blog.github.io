## Are Solar Panels Safe in Tordenvejr?

It’s natural to worry about your solceller til hus (solar panels for houses) during a heavy thunderstorm. Modern solcelleanlæg are designed to handle tough weather—including rain, hail, wind, and yes, lightning. However, while direct lightning strikes are rare, electrical surges from nearby strikes can potentially damage your system.

### How Can You Protect Your Solceller?

Most reputable installers, including EcoRay’s trusted partners, fit your system with surge protection and grounding. This means your investment is protected against voltage spikes, keeping your energy flowing safely. Always ask for transparent calculations and realistic savings estimates, so you know exactly what’s included in your solcelle pris (solar panel price).

## Common Questions about Thunderstorms and Solar

**What if lightning strikes my solar panels?**  
Direct strikes are rare. Proper installation with surge protection greatly reduces risk, and insurance may cover any unlikely damage.

**Do I need to turn off my system during tordenvejr?**  
In most cases, your intelligent solar system will manage itself automatically. For extra peace of mind, EcoRay can advise on best practices and automation options tailored to your region.
