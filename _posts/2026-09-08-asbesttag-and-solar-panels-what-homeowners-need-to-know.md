## What Is an Asbesttag and Why Does It Matter for Solar?

An asbesttag refers to a roof made with asbestos-containing materials, commonly used in Nordic homes built before the 1990s. While these roofs are durable, asbestos is now known to be hazardous—especially if the material is disturbed during renovations or installations. 

If you’re considering solceller til hus, understanding your roof’s composition is crucial. Solar installation on an asbesttag can be more complex and may require special handling or even a full roof replacement to ensure safety and compliance with current regulations in Denmark, Sweden, Norway, and even some U.S. states.

## Can You Install Solceller on an Asbesttag?

The short answer: direct installation is typically **not recommended**. Most reputable installers will advise against mounting solcelleanlæg on asbesttag due to health risks and regulatory restrictions. Here’s why:

- **Health risks:** Disturbing asbestos releases harmful fibers.
- **Legal requirements:** Many regions prohibit working directly with asbesttag without certified removal.
- **Insurance limitations:** Damage or issues from installing solceller on asbestos may not be covered.
