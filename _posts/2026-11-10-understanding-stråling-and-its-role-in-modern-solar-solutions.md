## What Is Stråling and Why Does It Matter for Solceller and Solar Investments?

Stråling is the energy from the sun that reaches the Earth’s surface as sunlight. This solar radiation is the essential ingredient that powers every solcelleanlæg (solar panel system). The stronger and more consistent the stråling, the more power your solceller can generate—meaning bigger savings on your electricity bill.

- **Homeowners:** The amount of stråling your roof receives directly impacts how much you’ll save with solceller til hus.
- **Businesses:** Commercial buildings with large, sunlit roofs are perfect for maximizing stråling and reducing operational costs.
