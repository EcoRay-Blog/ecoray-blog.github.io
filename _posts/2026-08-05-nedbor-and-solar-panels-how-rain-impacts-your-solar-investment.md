## How Nedbor Affects Solar Panel Performance

While it’s true that solar panels need sunlight to generate electricity, occasional nedbor is not a drawback—in fact, it can offer surprising benefits:

- **Self-Cleaning Effect:** Rain naturally washes away dust, pollen, and debris, keeping your solceller operating at peak efficiency. Clean panels can deliver higher output, helping you spar på elregning.
- **Cooling Benefit:** Cooler panels are more efficient. Nedbor helps reduce panel temperatures, which can slightly boost their performance compared to hot, dry conditions.
- **Long-Term Durability:** Consistent cleaning from rain can reduce maintenance needs and extend the lifespan of your solcelleanlæg.
